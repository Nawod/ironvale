# Building controls and zombie mode — 2026-10-15

Owned buildings now support relocation and permanent removal in single-player and online games. Original town centers stay fixed for conquest. Moving retains the same entity, health, wall tier, farm stock and worker targets. Sites can be removed before completion; movement requires completion. Empty queues/garrisons, clear scouted destinations, visible-enemy clearance and a ten-second damage cooldown prevent combat evasion. Removal has an inline confirmation and no refund. Existing worker task resumption handles deleted sites, including paused wall segments. Fog remembers a hidden moved building until the old site is scouted again.

Zombie mode is optional in solo setup, private rooms and public matchmaking. Classic/zombie and team/free-for-all queues are isolated, including cancelled countdown rematches. Host setting changes clear readiness. Neutral owner -2 ignores alliances, attacks every living empire at 18:00–06:00, breaches walls, and returns to its original forest at dawn without attacking. Zombies use no player population/resources or scouting and cannot capture cities. The normal conquest objective remains. Hordes refill once per night near real forest clusters: eight growing toward twelve per city, at most 96 globally. Mode, nightly cycle, refuges and building damage timestamps survive saves/checkpoints. Old saves remain compatible.

Undead reuse the shipped free Ashen alchemist body with gray-green skin, dark ragged clothing, reaching arms and shuffle/swipe poses. Shared full/crowd pose banks add one material; there are no per-zombie animation mixers or geometry copies. The procedural fallback and portrait also have a neutral undead appearance. Previous building models, city outfits, animals, nature and worker task memory remain active. No new external assets or paid content were downloaded.
