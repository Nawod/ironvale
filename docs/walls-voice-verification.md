# Wall alignment and voice chat — 2026-10-15

Walls, palisades and gates now face along adjacent defense runs. Their existing geometry remains unchanged. The renderer infers orientation from same-owner cardinal footprint adjacency in the player's snapshot, caches the map once per update, and reads one yaw per rendered building. It uses no hidden world information. Mixed gates, construction, known fog memories, relocation and removal are covered; corners and equal junctions have a stable original-axis default.

The online lobby and match share an opt-in audio-only WebRTC controller. Join starts muted. Free-for-all offers Open; teams offer Open and Team. Only participants in the same room and selected channel connect; Team additionally checks authoritative team membership. Channel switches disable capture and close old connections/audio elements before requesting new membership. Recipient/source epochs reject stale signals, including after tab replacement. Mute/unmute, independent incoming-audio mute, leave and autoplay recovery are available. Voice persists across lobby-to-match, and its controls remain above pause/settings/elimination/victory overlays. Leaving, disconnecting, replacement or microphone loss stops capture. Reconnection requires explicit rejoining. Team/mode changes end membership so players can rejoin the new arrangement. Audio and membership are not recorded or saved.

The server exposes bounded ICE configuration, defaults to public Cloudflare STUN, and supports optional configured TURN and built-in HTTPS using supplied PEM files. Microphones require localhost or browser-trusted HTTPS. Plain HTTP LAN play remains available but cannot access microphones. No relay was purchased, no external account was created, and no public deployment or browser trust changes were made.
