# Riverlands and living battlefields

The user approved the current buildings and requested continued progress toward detailed medieval cities and battles, using Age of Empires references. Preserve all approved building models, city colors, animal models, accessible HUD, teams, private resources, voice, optional zombies, worker tasks and fair AI costs/timers.

This phase adds a playable Riverlands map alongside the existing Lowlands, richer woodland silhouettes, and clearer movement/combat presentation. A cosmetic river without real navigation would misrepresent gameplay; a full naval economy would expand the scope unnecessarily. Use a deterministic river with permanent bridges and shallow crossings. Land armies must route through crossings, buildings cannot occupy water or crossings, and all starting cities must have safe land and connected routes. Resources, recruits, wildlife and zombies must stay on valid terrain. Existing saves without a map setting retain Lowlands behavior.

Expose `MapType = 'lowlands' | 'riverlands'` as optional `mapType` in match and online settings. Default omitted settings to Lowlands throughout older saves/protocol paths; new menus may recommend Riverlands explicitly. Terrain codes are grass 0, earth 1, woodland 2, deep water 3, ford 4, bridge 5. Add one shared terrain module for passability, footprint checks and deterministic generation. Crossings are neutral permanent map features, never owned buildings. Queue matching includes map choice so players receive their selected terrain. Validate settings on the server and when loading saves.

Water uses one lightweight animated surface with fog-aware rendering, shoreline colors and geometric bridge decks/rails matching the traversable tiles. No reflection render targets. Hide all terrain detail in unexplored tiles and dim remembered terrain. Minimap distinguishes water/crossings. Placement preview and authoritative building/relocation rules agree. Ground cover does not grow on water or decks. Share geometry/materials, bound cosmetics and dispose GPU resources.

Woodland improvements use existing licensed assets/textures and shared geometry; preserve original animal models and resource locations/hit targets. Army presentation should make walking, facing and attack actions clearer without changing combat timers or damage. No Age of Empires assets, new paid dependencies, external uploads or publishing.

Acceptance: deterministic connected Riverlands for 2–8 players and unequal teams; no water spawns or water construction/relocation; paths and formations respect water; old saves and Lowlands remain usable; map choice round-trips through private/public multiplayer and persistence. Validate simulation and network behavior, full types/build/tests, desktop and mobile browser flows, fog appearance, bridge routes and runtime errors. Record real screenshots and performance observations with their limits.
